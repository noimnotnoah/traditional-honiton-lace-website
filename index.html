For centuries, the lacemakers of Honiton have worked fine thread into heirloom pieces admired across the world. That tradition continues at 52 High Street — wedding lace, heirloom pieces, gifts and the tools of the craft.

01 / Heritage

A craft that made a town famous

Honiton lace has dressed royal weddings and heirloom christenings since the 16th century. Made by hand on lace pillows, stitch by patient stitch, it remains one of the finest laces in the world — and it is still made here, in Honiton.

Our shop carries on that line: handmade pieces, made by local lacemakers, alongside everything you need to learn the craft yourself.
